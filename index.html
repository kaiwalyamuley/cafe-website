Chai the way our neighbours make it

Ghat Side started as one stove and a row of steel glasses. We still boil chai in small pots so every batch tastes the same as the last one.

The milk comes from a dairy a few kilometres out of the city, the spices are ground every week, and the misal masala is made in our own kitchen. Nothing fancy, just done carefully.
